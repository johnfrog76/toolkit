## Context
This is the **Azure demo**: a Space ready for YOUR Azure DevOps organisation. There is no public one to
point it at, so it ships `local`, with no address, and contacts nothing until you connect it. Until then
it is cards like this one -- everything works from the files.

An Azure DevOps Space is an organisation, with projects inside it. Connecting is four steps, and the
wizard says which are yours: the tip at the foot of Home opens it, or type `Connect azure-demo` in a terminal.

## Summary
