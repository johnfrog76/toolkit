# Import a recipe from a URL

## Problem

Most recipes a cook wants already exist on a web page. Retyping a title, twenty ingredients and a dozen steps
is the reason people give up on keeping their recipes in an app.

## The idea

Paste a link and get a draft recipe back: the page is fetched, the recipe is found in it (most recipe pages
describe it in a machine-readable block), and the cook confirms the draft before it is saved.

## Open questions

- What do we do with a page that has no machine-readable recipe?
- Do we keep the address, so the recipe can say where it came from?
