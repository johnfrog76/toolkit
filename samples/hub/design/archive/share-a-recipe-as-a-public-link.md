# Share a recipe as a public link

## Problem

A cook wants to send a recipe to a friend. Today the options are a screenshot or retyping it.

## The idea

A public, read-only link to a recipe, which anyone can open without an account.

## Open questions

- Who can revoke a link, and how does the cook know who has seen it?
- Is a recipe the cook copied from someone else theirs to share?

## Source

Suggested in feedback from early users.
