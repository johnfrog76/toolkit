# Read a recipe from a photo

## Problem

Half the recipes people own are on paper: a card in a shoebox, a page torn from a magazine. Importing from a
link (the URL import) does nothing for them, so those recipes never reach the app.

## The idea

Photograph the page and get back a draft recipe -- title, ingredients with quantities, steps -- that the cook
only has to correct, never retype. The phone's camera is the input; the draft is the output; the cook stays
in charge of what is saved.

## Open questions

- How good is text recognition on a creased or handwritten card, and what do we show when it is unsure of a
  quantity?
- What does one photo cost to process, and is that a price we can absorb or a limit we must set?
- Does the draft keep the photo, so the cook can compare it with what was read?
