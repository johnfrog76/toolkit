## Summary
As the person who did the work, I want to say "look at this", so that someone else looks before it is
called done.

Review is a moment, and the card records it. On a project with a repository, opening a pull request
writes a `PR URL:` line and the card walks to **Waiting** by itself. With no repository at all, you say it
yourself.

## Done when
I am in **Waiting**. Drag me from To do to Waiting: that writes one `**Review:**` line, with today's date,
in the footer below -- open me afterwards and it is there. From a terminal, `Move R3VU -To CodeReview`
writes the same line.
