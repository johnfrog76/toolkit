- Empty a seat in one step: `Staff -Seat <seat> -Vacate`, or **Vacate this seat** in the Org tab's Change dialog,
  takes away the seat's model and session and keeps everything else. A position with one seat can now be named
  by its id alone (`Staff -Seat vp-product -Model fable`), and `OrgChart` ends with a count, `staffed: 4 of 10`.
